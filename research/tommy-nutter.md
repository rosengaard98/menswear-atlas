# Tommy Nutter (person)

## Claims
- Thomas Albert Nutter was born 17 April 1943 in Barmouth, Wales, and died 17 August 1992 at 49 of AIDS-related complications. He studied plumbing and architecture before leaving at 19 to learn tailoring at the Tailor and Cutter Academy. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Nutter)
- He worked at the Savile Row house Donaldson, Williams & Ward (spellings vary between sources), where he met the cutter Edward Sexton. Put This On calls him a salesman there. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Nutter); [Put This On](https://putthison.com/?p=39509)
- Nutters of Savile Row opened at 35a Savile Row in 1969. The Rake says St Valentine's Day 1969 and calls it the first new company on the Row in 100 years. — [The Rake](https://therake.com/stories/edwards-scissorhands-edward-sexton)
- Backers included Cilla Black, her husband Bobby Willis, Peter Brown (Beatles management) and the lawyer James Vallance-White. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Nutter)
- Three of the four Beatles on the *Abbey Road* cover wore Nutters suits. Lennon wore a cream corduroy Nutter jacket to his wedding to Yoko Ono, and the Jaggers wore Nutter clothes at their 1971 wedding. — [Mr Porter](https://www.mrporter.com/en-us/journal/fashion/meet-the-tailor-who-dressed-the-rolling-stones-538574)
- Other clients named: Elton John, Twiggy, Eric Clapton, Peter Sellers, David Hockney, Roy Strong, Diana Ross. — [Mr Porter](https://www.mrporter.com/en-us/journal/fashion/meet-the-tailor-who-dressed-the-rolling-stones-538574); [Put This On](https://putthison.com/?p=39509); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Nutter)
- House look: long, waisted, flared jackets with narrow square rope shoulders, very wide peak lapels on single-breasted coats, parallel-legged trousers; also cut for women from the start; dressed shop windows instead of a velvet curtain. — [The Rake](https://therake.com/stories/edwards-scissorhands-edward-sexton)
- Signature was a long hacking-style jacket with wide peak lapels, contrast trims and patch-like patterned cloth; Ringo's checked outfit had pagoda shoulders and broad shawl lapels. — [Mr Porter](https://www.mrporter.com/en-us/journal/fashion/meet-the-tailor-who-dressed-the-rolling-stones-538574)
- Put This On: hacking-jacket shapes, square shoulders, nipped waists, loud plaids, bias-cut patch pockets. *Punch* is quoted as calling it a mix of Lord Emsworth, the Great Gatsby and Bozo the Clown. — [Put This On](https://putthison.com/?p=39509)
- Sexton took over as leading shareholder in 1976, pushing Nutter out. Nutter then worked for Kilgour, French & Stanbury and in ready-to-wear for Austin Reed (Cue Shop, first collection 1979 per Archived Dreams). — [Archived Dreams](https://www.archiveddreams.com/tommy-nutter-savile-rows-greatest-renegade); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Nutter)
- He reopened as "Tommy Nutter, Savile Row" at No 19 (Wikipedia: 1983, backed by J&J Crombie; Archived Dreams: 1982, backed by Alan Lewis). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Nutter); [Archived Dreams](https://www.archiveddreams.com/tommy-nutter-savile-rows-greatest-renegade)
- Nutter reportedly designed Jack Nicholson's Joker suit for *Batman* (1989). — [Archived Dreams](https://www.archiveddreams.com/tommy-nutter-savile-rows-greatest-renegade); [Mr Porter](https://www.mrporter.com/en-us/journal/fashion/meet-the-tailor-who-dressed-the-rolling-stones-538574)
- Legacy: Edward Sexton's firm and Chittleborough & Morgan (run by a former Nutter employee) still sell refined versions of his cuts. Richard James, Ozwald Boateng and Tom Ford are cited as heirs. Lance Richardson's *House of Nutter* (2018) is the biography. — [Mr Porter](https://www.mrporter.com/en-us/journal/fashion/meet-the-tailor-who-dressed-the-rolling-stones-538574); [Put This On](https://putthison.com/?p=39509); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Nutter)

## Disagreements and myths
- Reopening: 1983 backed by Crombie (Wikipedia) versus 1982 backed by Alan Lewis (Archived Dreams). Possibly both, as backer and owner.
- Opening date: St Valentine's Day 1969 is The Rake's only; others say just 1969.
- Batman: Wikipedia marks the claim "citation needed"; Archived Dreams and Mr Porter state it.
- Where Nutter started: delivery/salesman (Put This On) versus other accounts (Wikipedia gives no role). Sexton started as a delivery boy per Archived Dreams.
- Cecil Gee and Hung On You, mentioned in the brief, appeared in none of the pages I opened; do not claim them.
- Wikipedia says Nutter was bought out in 1976; Archived Dreams says Sexton removed him as leading shareholder. Put This On says only that they fell out.
