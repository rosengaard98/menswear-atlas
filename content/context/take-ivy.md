*Take Ivy* was published in Japan in 1965 by Fujingahosha, today Hachette Fujingaho. Its four authors were Japanese enthusiasts of American clothing: Teruyoshi Hayashida, Shosuke Ishizu, Toshiyuki Kurosu and Hajime (Paul) Hasegawa. They photographed students on Ivy League campuses as the [[ivy-league-look]] was reaching the end of its mainstream run.

Two of them worked for the Japanese clothing company [[van-jacket|VAN Jacket]]: Kurosu joined in 1961 and Hasegawa in 1963, after studying in the United States.

## Second life

For Japanese baby boomers the book became the Ivy bible. In August 2010 powerHouse Books in Brooklyn published an English edition, and a Dutch translation followed in 2011. It has sold more than 50,000 copies, and it is credited with feeding the neo-Ivy revival of the mid-2010s.
