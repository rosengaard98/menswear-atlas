Preppy grew out of the [[ivy-league-look]] and overlaps with it so much that the line between them is blurred. It is more colourful, especially in spring and summer, and more relaxed.

The trigger was a joke. *The Official Preppy Handbook*, edited by Lisa Birnbach and published by Workman in 1980, satirised the habits of wealthy students at Ivy League and elite liberal arts colleges. The book helped revive interest in the clothes through the 1980s. Mass marketing by [[ralph-lauren|Ralph Lauren]], Lacoste, Izod and others moved the style into the mainstream before interest faded in the 1990s.

## The pieces

Navy blazers with brass buttons, [[ocbd|oxford button-downs]], repp-stripe and knitted silk ties, cable-knit and argyle sweaters, surcingle and woven leather belts, seersucker and madras, grey flannels, chinos, corduroys and Nantucket reds, penny loafers and buckskin shoes, and the polo shirts, rugby shirts and boat shoes of weekends.

## Since

In the 2010s many preps moved back towards the tailored original Ivy look, and in the early to mid-2020s the style returned under a new name, the old money aesthetic.
